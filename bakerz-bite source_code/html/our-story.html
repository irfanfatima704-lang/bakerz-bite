About Us

Where Smiles Are Served Daily.

The story of a small kitchen, a few good recipes and the people who bake them.

It started with one oven

Bakerz Bite began as a tiny home kitchen and a habit of baking for friends and family. Word spread one cake at a time, and the kitchen soon grew into the café you can visit in Gulberg, Lahore today.

We still bake in small batches and still treat every order like it is for someone we know.

Step by step

Our Journey

2019

A tiny kitchen

The first recipes: a red velvet cake, chocolate chip cookies and a lot of taste-testing by family.

2021

Orders by word of mouth

Neighbours became regulars, and birthday cakes became our speciality.

2023

The café opens

We opened our doors in Gulberg, Lahore, with a counter of pastries and a proper coffee machine.

Today

A bigger menu, the same care

From cakes and croissants to pizza, tea and gifts, with delivery across the cities we serve.
