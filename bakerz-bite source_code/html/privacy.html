Privacy Policy

How we handle your information.

What this site stores

Bakerz Bite has no backend or database. Your shopping cart, light/dark theme choice, visit counter and any reviews you write are saved only in your own browser using localStorage. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings.

Forms

The contact form and newsletter box do not ask for payment-card information. Card details entered at checkout are used only to pay for your order and are not stored.

Location

The bottom bar can show approximate coordinates, but only after you press “Share location” and allow it in your browser. Coordinates are shown on screen and are not saved or transmitted by this site.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics. The visit counter is local to your browser and is not a global count.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the Contact page.
